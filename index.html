Background

Before joining NVIDIA in November 2025, I spent over fifteen years at Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory. I led PASSION Lab, a research team of UC Berkeley Ph.D. students and LBNL postdoctoral fellows, research scientists, and engineers.

I also directed Sparsitute, a six-institution DOE mathematical center with more than 30 members, and served on the UC Berkeley EECS adjunct faculty from 2017 to 2025.

I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science from UC Santa Barbara in 2010, advised by John R. Gilbert, and my B.S. from Sabancı University in 2005.
