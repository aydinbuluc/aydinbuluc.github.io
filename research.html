01 / Sparse computation

Sparse matrices,
tensors, and solvers

I primarily work on our next-generation iterative solvers (cuISS), but I also frequently collaborate with cuDSS and cuSPARSE teams.

My broader research includes parallel sparse matrix multiplication, sparse matrix-vector multiplication, sparse tensors, distributed data structures, and communication-avoiding algorithms. A recurring goal is to reduce data movement while exposing parallelism across cores, GPUs, and distributed-memory systems.

These methods support scientific computing, graph analysis, and parallel algorithms for machine learning, including communication-efficient graph neural network training.

02 / Graph algorithms

Linear algebra as
a language for graphs

GraphBLAS C API & standards

I made substantial technical contributions to the GraphBLAS C API as a member of the original five-person C API team. Through steering committee membership, I also contributed to the strategic direction of the broader effort, connecting interface design, algorithms, and implementations across academia, national laboratories, and industry.

03 / Computational biology

From sparse computation
to protein families

I conceived and led the design of HipMCL, a scalable parallel implementation of Markov clustering, and PASTIS, a distributed-memory protein sequence similarity tool based on sparse matrix operations.

Together, HipMCL and PASTIS helped more than double the number of known protein families in our global metagenomics study, published in Nature in 2023.

View the publication →

I also contributed to the HipMer/MetaHipMer genome assembly effort and to protein-family identification and genome assembly at supercomputing scale within ExaBiome.

Research leadership

Collaborative science

Sparsitute

While at LBNL, I was founding lead PI and Center Director of Sparsitute, a DOE mathematical center spanning six institutions and more than 30 members. I set the technical agenda and coordinated research in sparse computation and software.

I served as LBNL PI for ExaGraph and led research programs in scalable graph learning, randomized sparse algorithms, and energy-efficient parallel graph and data mining algorithms.
