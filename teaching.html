CS267
Applications of Parallel Computers

I modernized CS267 with multiple lectures on parallel algorithms for machine learning and expanded coverage of distributed-memory programming, graph algorithms, sorting and searching, and computational biology.

I co-taught the course with Jim Demmel, Katherine Yelick, and Laura Grigori across different years.
